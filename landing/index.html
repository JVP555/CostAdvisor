How it works

From raw materials
to negotiation brief.

  1. 01
    Build your cost model. Compose each product from its commodity components — PE, naphtha, energy, logistics. Assign weights. CostAdvisor links each component to a live index automatically.
  2. 02
    Indices update every night. Nightly pulls from ECB, EIA, Eurostat, FRED, and World Bank keep your should-cost current. No spreadsheets, no manual refresh. Override any index with your own data.
  3. 03
    Walk in with a brief. One click generates your negotiation brief: should-cost vs. actual, ranked drivers, squeeze zones, and an AI narrative. Export as PDF.
