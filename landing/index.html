How it works

From raw materials
to negotiation brief.

  1. 01
    Decompose your product. Break each product into its commodity components — feedstock, energy, labor, logistics. Assign cost weights. CostAdvisor links each component to a live public index automatically.
  2. 02
    Indices update every night. Nightly pulls from ECB, EIA, Eurostat, FRED, and World Bank keep your should-cost current. No spreadsheets, no manual refresh. Override any index with your own data or team sources.
  3. 03
    Compare against actuals. Upload your supplier's prices. CostAdvisor calculates the gap — the difference between what the market justifies and what you're paying — quarter by quarter.
  4. 04
    Walk in with a brief. One click generates your negotiation brief: verdict, ranked cost drivers, total financial impact, and an AI narrative. Export as PDF — named for the product, supplier, and quarter.
