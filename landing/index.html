How it works

From raw materials to negotiation brief in three steps

01

Build your cost model

Compose each product's cost from its commodity components — PE, naphtha, energy, logistics. Assign weights. Set your base price and margin. CostAdvisor links each component to a live index automatically.

→
02

Indices update automatically

Nightly pulls from ECB, EIA, Eurostat, FRED, and World Bank keep your should-cost current. No spreadsheets, no manual refresh. Bring your own team data sources or override any index with proprietary figures.

→
03

Walk in with a brief

One click generates your negotiation brief: should-cost vs. actual, ranked cost drivers, squeeze and desqueeze zones, and an AI narrative. Export as PDF and hand it to your supplier.

Capabilities

Everything you need to own the number

⬡

Should-Cost Modeling

Build weighted cost formulas from commodity indices. The model updates when markets move — no re-entry required. Supports INCOTERM-normalised landed costs.

◈

Live Market Indices

ECB, EIA, Eurostat, FRED, World Bank — scraped every night. FX rates included. Add your own team data sources for proprietary index data.

◇

Quarterly Evolution

See how should-cost has moved quarter by quarter against actuals. Identify the moment your supplier started over-pricing — with receipts.

▲

Squeeze Analysis

Squeeze and desqueeze calculations show exactly where your supplier has captured or absorbed margin over time. Quantify the gap before you sit down.

✦

AI Negotiation Brief

Gap analysis, ranked drivers, total financial impact, and a narrative written for a supplier conversation — generated in one click, ready to share.

⬧

PDF Export

Print-ready brief with verdict, gap, top drivers, and evolution chart. Named for the product, supplier, and quarter. Hand it across the table or send it in advance.
