"Index data-quality validation over the resolution layer (declared + structural contradictions first)"

Flagging index values that look wrong, built on the layer that makes 'wrong' checkable.

The original framing — compare the same series across two sources and flag the outlier — assumes we hold two independent readings of one series. Mostly we do not. What the drop does supply is a large amount of declared and structural disagreement that is sitting in the data unexamined, and that is both cheaper to check and more actionable:

_issues.csv is a delivered known-defect list, keyed table,key,column,problem. It is an input to load, not something to re-derive. SCRUM-100's 'check every formula's index code exists' is already answered by it.

proxy_status contradicts itself. A substantial block of indexed cost lines carry a combo_lines.proxy_status that disagrees with the proxy_status on their own type_codes row. This is a real, mechanical, checkable contradiction and it exists today.

type_codes.resolution carries ambiguous as a first-class state alongside resolved and no_series — a code we cannot confidently point at one series.

Provenance is declared per card and per series: index_feeds.csv has agency, source_freq, sourcing_note, is_default_region; index_commodities.csv has agency, source_url, label_unit, label_incoterm. Where a card and its series disagree about agency, unit or incoterm, that is a contradiction worth surfacing. Several series are re-badged with a foreign region, some carry explicit 'Unverified' agency markers, and there is a body of methodology flags.

Two shipped constraints reject valid drop data on sight: CommodityIndex.provider is String(64) and a couple of agency strings exceed it; app/constants/index_metadata.py::FREQUENCIES rejects several frequency values present in the drop. Both are load-time failures, so they need resolving as part of this rather than discovered during it.

Statistical cross-checking of two readings of one series stays in scope as a later mode, for the series where we actually hold two. It is not the first thing to build, and it cannot be built before DB-5 exists — there is no resolution layer to cross-check against today.

This blocks on DB-5. Do not start it first.

Done when

A validation run over the loaded index layers emits structured findings, each naming the table, key, column and the two conflicting values.

A type-code whose proxy_status disagrees with the proxy_status on its cost lines appears as a finding.

A card whose declared agency, unit or incoterm disagrees with its series appears as a finding.

Findings from _issues.csv are carried through rather than recomputed, and are distinguishable from findings we derived.

A series with forecast points and no actual history is flagged, and the flag is queryable rather than log-only.

The run is inspectable after the fact and re-runnable without duplicating findings.
