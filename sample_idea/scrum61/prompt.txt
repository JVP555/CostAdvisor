This is the first time the app is laid out the way a buyer actually works — you start from the raw public price feeds and walk all the way to a supplier negotiation — so the shell is what turns a pile of separate screens into one path. Indexes goes first because those public feeds are the ground truth underneath every should-cost we produce; everything downstream is built on them. A buyer only really cares about the handful their own products depend on though, not all 158, so the tab auto-follows just those — and it shows a multi-year history, because what matters is where a feed has been trending, not just where it ticked today.
