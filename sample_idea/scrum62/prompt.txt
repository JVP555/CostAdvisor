A category manager doesnt really think in indexes or formulas day to day — they think about the products theyre on the hook to buy well. Portfolio is their home base for exactly that: every product they own in one place, each carrying its own live should-cost they can actually act on. Right now the product isnt really the centre of anything — the formula and its starting point float around beside it — so theres no single screen where a buyer just sees 'this is my product, and heres what it should cost right now.' Making the product the thing everything hangs off of is what turns a pile of indexes into a portfolio someone actually manages.

Rebuild Portfolio with the product as the central object (formula + starting point + should-cost are its properties); should-cost is always live.

First-class starting point per product.

Wire to existing should-cost engine outputs only.
