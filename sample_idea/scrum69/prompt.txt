"index_seasonal_factor — generate the seasonality, do not import it"

Seasonality is a derived table, not editorial content. In the drop the seasonality factors reproduce digit-for-digit from the forecast index series, and the accompanying season notes are a small set of templates rendering those same factors with zero mismatches between text and number.

So: compute them. index_seasonal_factor(commodity_id, region, month, factor, method, window_months, computed_at) with the method recorded on the row, recomputed when the series updates.

This epic owns producing the table. SCRUM-75 / INT-1 blends these per combo for the card's seasonality curve; SCRUM-74's index data layer carries only the do-not-import rule. Nobody else generates a second set.

Imported as prose instead, the factors freeze at the values they had on the day of the drop, disagree with the series within a quarter or two, and there is no way to tell which of the two the app should believe.

Done when

Factors are computed from the stored series rather than read from the payload, and the method is recorded on the row.

Recompute is idempotent and safe to re-run.

The generated factors reproduce the drop's values within tolerance for the series that have full history — that is the regression check that the method matches what the source used.

The season-note prose is rendered from the computed factors, so text and number cannot disagree.
