"DB-5 · Three-layer index model — schema, migration, retire the region collapse"

Split CommodityIndex into the three layers the data actually has: price series (region baked into the key), index card (display grouping, may share a series with other cards), and type code (the resolution join that cost lines name).

Reference shape is the drop's tables — newDocsFolder/seed/tables/index_commodities.csv, index_feeds.csv, type_codes.csv. Columns worth carrying through by name: on the type-code layer resolves_to, resolution, proxy_status, swap_priority, ideal_index, registry_note; on the card layer series_key, shares_series_with, is_default_region, region_label; on the series layer value_kind, quoted_incoterm, quoted_named_place, agency.

Retire the region-stripping in seed_index_metadata._new_base_and_region() (line 71) — the trailing token on a type-code is often a source, not a region, which is why the collapse mis-files feeds.

FormulaTemplateComponent.commodity_id currently FKs straight at commodity_indexes.id. Deciding whether a cost line points at a type-code or keeps pointing at a series with the type-code recorded alongside is the central call in this subtask, and it determines whether the resolution chain is queryable or has to be reassembled.

Tenancy is unsettled and needs settling here: commodity_indexes has no team_id and no RLS, while IndexOverride and TeamIndexSource key on (commodity_id, region) — a composite that stops describing anything once region is in the series key.

Done when migrations exist for all three layers; a type-code's full chain to its series is retrievable in one query; a card that shares a series with another card is representable; and seed_index_metadata no longer derives a region by splitting a code.
