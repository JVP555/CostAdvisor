"DB-7 · Index dossier storage + platform volatility calibration"



Storage for the structured half of an index dossier: upstream drivers with correlation coefficient, lag and signal direction; the price-transmission chain; methodology flags; roles; the index-specific role a producer or price-setter plays; supply/demand splits; cycle position; negotiation pointers; volatility.

Three boundaries to hold:

Computed snapshots are not dossier content. index_feeds.csv ships current_value, change_pct, volatility_pct, cycle_pct, card_status, has_intel_block. Those are moments in time, recomputable from the series. docs/CONTENT_MODEL.md states the rule — never store a derived value as prose if it can be computed. The same reasoning retires INDEX_SEASONALITY and INDEX_SEASON_NOTES as import candidates: they reproduce from the series digit-for-digit. Producing index_seasonal_factor is SCRUM-69's; the only rule kept here is that it is generated, never imported.

Prose is not here. Narratives, season notes and proxy notes key to index slug and are the second subject_type in W3-B's editorial_block. This subtask stores the structured fields only.

Company records are not here. SCRUM-77 owns the producer entity and its alias layer (coordinated with W3-D SUP-1/SUP-2). This subtask stores the index-specific role and references the producer by FK, so there is one master rather than two.

The volatility percentile calibration is platform-level, computed across the whole library, and must be recomputed when the library changes. It lives here — the stored ladder, the recompute path and the test are all this subtask's. SCRUM-75 reads the ladder to place a combo's dispersion on it and reports which calibration it read; it does not recompute.

Done when the structured dossier fields are storable and retrievable per series (and per card where they differ by region); a driver row carries its correlation, lag and signal together; a producer role resolves to SCRUM-77's producer entity by FK rather than storing the company inline; the volatility calibration is stored platform-level with a recompute path; and the recompute is exercised by a test that adds a series and asserts the breakpoints move.
