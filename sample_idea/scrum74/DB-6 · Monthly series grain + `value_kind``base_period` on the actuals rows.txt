"DB-6 · Monthly series grain + `value_kind``base_period` on the actuals rows"

IndexValue is (commodity_id, region, year, quarter). The drop's series are monthly (index_series.csv: series_key, year, month, quarter, value, kind) and the quarterly files are rollups derived from them, not the source.

Two pieces:

Monthly grain, with the quarterly view derived rather than stored twice. index_values.csv has no region column at all and region is only recoverable through the card layer, where a block of cards carry region='multi' — so plan for region to come from the series key, not from a column on the value row.

value_kind and base_period stored and served. Every series is index_level, base 100 = Jan 2023 — nothing here is a currency price, so the should-cost-vs-actual gap cannot be expressed in money from this data alone. Storing the kind explicitly is what stops the engines and the UI implying money that is not there.

Forecast storage is SCRUM-70's. kind carries actual-vs-forecast in-band in the drop; the forecast table, the stored discriminator and the retrofit onto the existing costing path belong there, and this subtask consumes the discriminator rather than defining it. Agree the column names with whoever takes SCRUM-70 before writing the migration. One thing worth handing over rather than absorbing: some stub series carry forecast points with no actual history behind them, and that has to stay distinguishable from a trajectory.

Watch the temporal carry-forward at the bottom of data_resolver.get_single_index_value (lines 387-403): it exists so future reference quarters do not flatten ratios to 1.0, and it will happily carry a value forward across a boundary where a forecast should be serving instead. Worth deciding what it does once forecasts are real.

Done when monthly values load and are queryable at monthly grain; the quarterly view derives from them rather than being stored alongside them; and value_kind + base_period appear in the API response.
