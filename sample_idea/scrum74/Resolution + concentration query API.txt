"Resolution + concentration query API"


The point of the three-layer model is that questions we currently cannot ask become answerable from the DB. This subtask is those questions as endpoints.

Today the type-code → series resolution happens in memory inside seed_combos.feed_code_map() (line 238) and is discarded when the load finishes. The consequence is concrete: one series backs roughly a quarter of all indexed cost weight, reached through dozens of separate type-codes, and nothing in the app can see it. A buyer looking at a diversified-looking cost breakdown is looking at one number wearing many labels.

The questions:

What does this type-code resolve to, through what, and is that a proxy?

Which type-codes resolve to this series, and what share of indexed cost weight do they carry?

Why can't this combo be costed? — naming the specific lines and the specific reason (no_series, ambiguous, resolved but no price history).

What breaks if this series goes away or gets re-sourced?

GET /indexes/{commodity_id}/impact is the nearest existing thing, but it walks team CostModel / FormulaComponent rows, so it answers 'which of my cost models use this' and not 'what does the platform library depend on'. Different question, different join.

Two known consumers, so it is worth designing the response for them: SCRUM-80 layers swap-backlog ranking and derivation provenance on these reads, and SCRUM-71 rolls up index exposure through this endpoint rather than aggregating the chain itself.

Done when GET on a type-code returns its full chain including for a code resolving through a proxy and for one whose resolution is no_series; the reverse lookup on a series returns its type-codes with their cost-weight share; an endpoint given an unpriceable combo names the blocking lines and the reason per line; and the responses distinguish resolved, no_series and ambiguous rather than collapsing the last two.
