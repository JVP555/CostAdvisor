"Index layer loader v2"

Load the three-layer index tables, idempotently, with a readable diff report.

Decisions this loader cannot avoid making, so better made deliberately:

Which proxy_status is authoritative. A substantial block of indexed cost lines carry a combo_lines.proxy_status that disagrees with the proxy_status on their own type_codes row, and FormulaTemplateComponent.is_proxy is already a third copy in the DB. Whichever the loader reads, the others contradict it. Pick one as the source and derive the rest.

resolution has three states — resolved, no_series, ambiguous. Two-state handling silently mis-files the third.

FormulaRegionCoverage uniqueness. UniqueConstraint(template_id, region) (uq_frc_template_region) rejects the second of any pair of combos differing only by combos.variant, and the drop contains such pairs. Hard failure at load.

Two shipped constraints reject valid data: CommodityIndex.provider is String(64) and a couple of agency strings exceed it; app/constants/index_metadata.py::FREQUENCIES rejects several frequency values present in the drop.

coverage_tier is two metrics. The shipped column means worst retrieval tier among inputs (RETRIEVAL_STATUSES); the drop's combos.coverage_tier is proxy density (P1/P2/P3). Both are useful — two columns, not a mapping.

Margin sits inside the 100. combos.weight_total is exactly 100 on every combo with lines. Any weight validation this loader does has to check for exactly 100; the old 99.5–110.5 band was built for margin-on-top and passes a double-counted margin without a warning. Margin is also stated twice in the source and the two disagree on a real slice of combos — the line weight is the safer default because it belongs to a set summing to 100.

Before writing anything: seed_catalog.resolve_workbook() globs seed-data-reference*.xlsx and takes the newest by mtime, so a new workbook left anywhere visible silently redirects the shipped loader at incompatible data. Whether seed_catalog / seed_combos are retargeted or retired is a Phase-0 decision (docs/WAVE3_PLAN.md §0) and should be closed first.

Done when the loader is idempotent (second run produces an empty diff); it emits a per-table diff report naming inserts, updates and skips with reasons; a contradicting proxy_status is resolved by a stated rule and the losing value is recorded rather than dropped; combos differing only by variant both load; and _issues.csv findings are carried through rather than recomputed.
